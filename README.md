# Awesome-Education-Analytics

# Top Education Analytics Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Student Success, Learning Analytics, Institutional Effectiveness & Data-Informed Instruction*  
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Education Analytics**. These tools help K-12 districts, higher education institutions, and educators transform scattered student data into actionable insights—identifying at-risk learners, measuring intervention effectiveness, and supporting data-informed decision-making at every level.

**Examples** include Civitas Learning, Otus, Schoolzilla, PowerSchool Unified Insights, Tableau for Education, D2L Performance+, BrightBytes, Watermark Insights, LearnPlatform, and SchoolStatus (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom data dashboards, and transparent education data management—ideal for institutions that need full control over sensitive student data without per-seat SaaS fees or vendor lock-in.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Civitas Learning](https://www.civitaslearning.com/)**
  Higher education analytics platform with institution-specific predictive models. Surfaces real-time insights on retention, persistence, completion, and career readiness. Provides AI assistants, holistic student profiles, and initiative analysis to measure what works .

- **[Otus](https://otus.com/)**
  K-12 assessment, data, and insights platform unifying assessment, grading, progress monitoring, and student data in one place. Named "Learning Analytics Solution of the Year" in 2025 EdTech Breakthrough Awards. Incorporates AI for streamlined data analysis and personalized recommendations .

- **[Schoolzilla](https://schoolzilla.com/)**
  Multi-measure dashboard for K-12 districts (now part of Renaissance). Connects attendance, behavior, and academic data from 80+ systems into a data warehouse. Provides Mosaic dashboards and Insight Reports for tracking progress against goals, identifying student groups for intervention, and monitoring at-risk indicators .

- **[PowerSchool Unified Insights](https://www.powerschool.com/)**
  K-12 analytics platform with 60+ research-based dashboards. Delivers role-based data access, longitudinal trend analysis, enhanced report cards, and holistic student profiles connecting attendance, academics, behavior, and SEL .

- **[Tableau for Education](https://www.tableau.com/)**
  Data visualization and business intelligence platform used widely in higher education. Institutions use Tableau for institutional research, student success analytics, and operational dashboards. Faculty and students eligible for free 1-year licenses .

- **[D2L Performance+](https://www.d2l.com/)**
  Learning analytics add-on for D2L Brightspace. Offers dashboards, reports, and predictive tools to uncover insights, track engagement, and identify at-risk learners. Reduces probability of human error through reproducible, reliable data workflows .

- **[BrightBytes](https://www.brightbytes.net/)**
  SaaS data analytics platform measuring technology use and linking it to learning outcomes. Integrates data from 100+ education systems. Chrome Agent collects usage data for identified educational applications with encrypted transmission .

- **[Watermark Insights](https://www.watermarkinsights.com/)**
  Higher education analytics platform with Leader Insights dashboard. Tracks student success and engagement data including enrollment, predicted risk for course completion, persistence risk, and key engagement distribution across risk levels. Supports institutional effectiveness and continuous improvement .

- **[LearnPlatform by Instructure](https://www.instructure.com/)**
  EdTech effectiveness platform helping districts evaluate, track, and maximize educational technology investments. Provides insights into tool usage and impact, helping administrators prioritize investments and ensure compliance .

- **[SchoolStatus](https://www.schoolstatus.com/)**
  K-12 MTSS and communication platform centralizing attendance, assessment, academic, and behavioral data. Supports tiered intervention (universal, targeted, intensive), predicts chronic absenteeism, and enables personalized family communication across email, SMS, phone, and print .

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[OpenEMIS](https://www.openemis.org/)**
  Education Management Information System designed to manage entire education systems. Collects and reports data on student attendance, behavior, and progress; teacher qualifications; and institutional data. No licensing fees, community-driven development. Features OpenEMIS Core, School, and Exams modules. Interoperable via open data standards, supports offline apps for remote areas. GNU GPL v3 .

- **[Student Performance Dashboard (hcoco1)](https://github.com/hcoco1/hcoco1-dashboard)**
  Interactive dashboard for visualizing and analyzing high school student grades over time. Built with Python, Dash, and Plotly with Bootstrap styling. Features student selection, year filtering, subject performance views, grade averages, detailed exam scores, and performance trend visualization. Bilingual (English/Spanish) .

- **[Apache Superset](https://github.com/apache/superset)**
  Open-source modern data exploration and visualization platform. Can be deployed for education analytics dashboards, connecting to student information systems, assessment platforms, and data warehouses. SQL-based querying with rich visualization library and role-based access.

- **[Metabase](https://github.com/metabase/metabase)**
  Open-source business intelligence tool that lets anyone ask questions and learn from data. Simple setup, no SQL required for basic queries. Can be connected to education data sources for self-service analytics and dashboards.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Data Warehousing**: **Apache Airflow** for orchestration, **dbt** for data transformation, **PostgreSQL** for storage.
- **Visualization Libraries**: **Plotly** (interactive charts), **D3.js** (custom visualizations), **Grafana** (dashboards).
- **Learning Management**: **Moodle** (open-source LMS with built-in analytics), **Canvas LMS** (open-core).

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Metabase** or **Apache Superset** for dashboards, **Airflow + dbt** for data pipelines, **PostgreSQL** for storage, and **Plotly** for custom visualizations. Add **OpenEMIS** for full education management information system capabilities.
